    Linked Accounts¶

    Someone who gets alerts on both Discord and Telegram has two PoracleNG accounts, and PoracleNG treats them as two people. Linking pairs one Discord account with one Telegram account so that PoracleWeb.NET treats them as one person: one list of alarms, the app chosen per alarm, and personal settings saved to both.

    Linking never copies or moves alarms, and unlinking deletes nothing. Settings are copied only when the site creates your other account for you (see the settings below). For how the two apps differ and how people register on each, see Discord and Telegram. For how the pair is stored and resolved, see the technical page.

    Linking¶

    Open Linked accounts from the user menu or under Settings in the sidebar. There's a card for each app, and what it offers depends on how you signed in:

    • Signed in with Telegram. Press Link Discord, approve the sign-in on Discord, and you come back to the page with the link made.
    • Signed in with Discord. Use the Telegram button on the Telegram card and approve it in Telegram.

    An app is offered only when its sign-in is set up on the site and switched on. When it isn't, its card says this site doesn't offer that app's sign-in, so it can't be linked here. An account can be in one link at a time, and only personal accounts link: a channel, group, thread or webhook never does. While an admin is viewing the site as you, the page says the link is yours to manage and offers nothing.

    If a link is refused¶

    The page says why, and what to try next. Dismiss the message with its close button.

    Message starts What happened
    Linking accounts has been switched off on this site An admin turned linking off while you were linking. Nothing was changed
    You can only link a Discord account to a Telegram account The account isn't a personal Discord or Telegram account, or both sides are the same app
    That account couldn't be read The id Discord or Telegram sent can't be an account id, or the Telegram login didn't verify
    That account is already used here as a channel or group That id is registered here as a destination, not a person
    That account is already linked to someone else Unlink it from the other account first
    That sign-in took too long A Telegram login is good for ten minutes after you approve it, and a Discord link for ten minutes after you press Link Discord. Start again
    This site doesn't offer sign-in with that app That app's sign-in isn't set up on this site, or an admin switched it off. Ask the site team if you expected it
    That account can't sign in to this site The account fails this site's own sign-in rules, such as a required Discord role
    You cancelled the link You backed out of the Discord approval. Nothing was changed
    The accounts couldn't be linked Something none of the above covers, or the link was lost part way. Nothing was changed; try again
    Your other account couldn't be set up The site tried to create the other account and couldn't. Nothing was linked

    Each side's state¶

    Each card says where that account stands.

    State Meaning
    You're signed in with this account The account you're using now, before anything is linked
    Not linked No account on this app is linked yet
    Receiving alerts The account exists and Poracle is sending it alerts
    Waiting to register The account is linked but Poracle doesn't know it yet. Alerts on that app start once you register there
    Alerts stopped You stopped alerts on that account. Start them in that app
    Paused by an admin An admin blocked the account. The card offers Get help when the site has a support link

    PoracleWeb.NET never turns a stopped or paused account back on. That's for the person or the admins on that app.

    Waiting to register on Telegram. Join the server's Telegram registration group and send /poracle there. If the admins set a registration group link, the card shows a Join the Telegram group button. Press Start in a chat with the bot as well, or it can't DM you.

    Waiting to register on Discord. Register the way the site's admins ask, usually by getting a role on the community's Discord server. The card shows Get help when the site has a support link.

    Where new alarms go¶

    Once both accounts are registered, New alarms go to picks the app for alarms you add. It's a default for new alarms only; existing alarms stay where they are, and every dialog lets you choose differently. An app that isn't receiving alerts is marked "(not receiving alerts)" and can't be picked. If it's already the default, the page says nothing sent there will arrive and suggests the other app. While one side is still waiting to register, the page says the choice comes once both are registered.

    Applying any quick pick creates its alarms on the default app, or on the account you're signed in with while the default app isn't registered.

    Alarms on both apps¶

    Every alarm list shows the alarms of both accounts together. Each card names the app it goes to, with "alerts off" when that app isn't receiving alerts, and Delivered to: All / Discord / Telegram above the list filters by app.

    Every add and edit dialog asks Which app gets this alarm? above its tabs. A new alarm starts on the default app. Picking the other app in an edit moves the alarm there when you save.

    To move several, tick them in select mode and choose Deliver to… (on a phone it's under More). An app every ticked alarm is already on is greyed out. The result counts what moved, what was already there, and what wasn't moved and why.

    How a move works¶

    A move creates the alarm on the other account first and deletes the original only once that worked, so an alarm is never lost on the way. If the delete fails, both copies stay and the message says so: "Moved to Telegram, but the Discord copy is still there. Delete it from the list."

    A move is refused, with nothing changed, when:

    Reason shown What to do
    Discord and Telegram are on different profiles Switch one app's active profile to match, then move again. The Linked accounts page offers this as a difference to fix
    The place "Home" isn't saved on Telegram Save that place on the other app first, or match saved places on Linked accounts
    It's limited to an area Telegram can't use Change the alarm's areas, then move it
    Telegram already has an alarm like it Telegram has an alarm that differs from this one only in its radius, template or clean setting. Edit or delete one of them, then move again. An identical alarm isn't refused: the move counts it as done and removes this copy
    Telegram isn't set up yet The other side is still waiting to register
    Alerts can't be moved while inspecting another account An admin viewing as you can't move your alarms

    Settings saved to both apps¶

    Saving any of these on the site saves it to both accounts: location, saved places, areas, profiles (names, schedules, areas and location), language, quiet periods, quest summary schedules, and alerts on or off.

    The account you signed in with is saved first. If the other app refuses or doesn't answer, what you saved stands on the first app and you see one message naming both, such as "Saved for Discord, but not for Telegram: the alert service didn't answer. Try again in a minute. Review the difference on Linked accounts." Its Review button opens Linked accounts, where the difference is waiting. Nothing is rolled back.

    When the two accounts differ¶

    Poracle's bot commands change one account only. The site can't stop the two from drifting, so it shows the drift and lets you choose.

    A worked example. You're linked, and both apps are set to Old Town. On the bus you send /location with a pin in Riverside to the Telegram bot. Telegram's account now measures distances from Riverside; Discord's still uses Old Town. Next time you open a page on the site, a one-line banner under the toolbar says "Discord and Telegram have different settings." with Review. On Linked accounts the Location line reads "Discord's is in Old Town, Telegram's is in Riverside, 4 km apart." Under Use Discord's it says "Telegram moves to Discord's location."; under Use Telegram's, "Discord moves to Telegram's location." You press Use Telegram's, a message confirms "Discord now has Telegram's location.", and the line goes.

    What's compared¶

    Line What it names
    Location Both places at the same level of detail and the distance between them. Never raw coordinates
    Areas The areas only one app has, in Poracle's spelling
    Profiles Profiles only one app has, and for shared ones whether the name, schedule or areas differ
    Active profile The profile each app is using, by name
    Saved places Places only one app has, and places in different spots

    Language, quiet periods and quest summary schedules are saved to both but not compared. Quiet periods, for one, live only in Poracle's memory and vanish when it restarts, so they'd show as different for no reason.

    Choosing a side¶

    Each line has Use Discord's and Use Telegram's, and under each button what it changes on the other app. Nothing is ever resolved for you.

    • A choice that deletes a profile or saved place from the other app asks first, naming what goes ("Delete Weekend from Telegram").
    • If that deletes the profile the other app is using, the line says that app switches profile, so its areas and location change too.
    • A profile that still holds alarms on the other app isn't deleted, and neither is a saved place that alarms there still use. The message names the app and what to change first.
    • The active profile can only be matched once the other app has that profile, so Profiles comes first in the list.
    • If either app changed since the list was drawn, you're told "That changed in the meantime", nothing is overwritten, and the list shows what differs now.
    • If a choice stops part way, the page says so and the list shows what still differs.

    Dismiss on the banner hides it for the rest of the browser session. A new or changed difference shows it again, and the list on Linked accounts always shows everything. The banner appears when a page opens, never in the middle of one, and isn't shown on Linked accounts itself or while an admin is viewing as you.

    Profile 0¶

    Some older accounts have a profile 0. Poracle never gives a new profile that number, so the other account can't have one. The pair leaves profile 0 out: it isn't compared, copied, renamed, switched to or deleted, and its alarms stay on the account that has it. The page says which account has one, for example "Your Discord account has an older profile 0 that Telegram can't have. Its alarms stay on Discord."

    Unlinking¶

    Press Unlink on the page and confirm. Both accounts and all their alarms stay; they just stop being treated as one person. Admins can do the same from Admin > Users, where a linked account has a chip naming the other app and its row menu has Unlink accounts.

    On a site that offers only one app's sign-in, unlinking is one way. You can't link the other app again or reach its alarms from this site afterwards, so the confirm says so: move any alarms you want to keep to the app you're signed in with first. Admin > Server settings > Linked accounts names an app people can't link and why (not set up in .env, or its Enable … login switch is off).

    Settings¶

    The settings are under Admin > Server settings > Linked accounts and in Site settings.

    Setting Key Default What it does
    Linked accounts disable_account_linking On Off hides the page and refuses every link route. Each person acts as their single account again. Links are kept for when it's switched back on, and Admin > Users can still unlink them
    Register the other account when linking link_register_missing Off Off saves the link and the missing side waits. When qualified creates a missing Telegram account only if Poracle's own /poracle rule would admit the person (see below); a missing Discord account still waits. Always creates the missing account straight away. A created account gets the person's language, location, the areas they may select, profiles (on the same numbers) and saved places. Alarms are never copied. If creating it fails, nothing is linked and nothing is left behind. Sites that stored the old on/off switch keep their behaviour: on reads as Always, off as Off
    Telegram registration group link telegram_registration_url Empty The invite link people use to register on Telegram, such as https://t.me/+abc123. Must be https:// on t.me or telegram.me. Shown as Join the Telegram group while a Telegram side is waiting

    A created account skips that app's registration rules but not its later checks. Poracle removes an account it doesn't think should exist only when the server runs its role check with role_check_mode set to disable-user or delete; with Poracle's defaults the account stays. See Telegram.

    Let Poracle's channel admins manage here (delegation_poracle_channels) sits near these settings but is about destinations, not linking. See Destinations & delegates.

    When qualified¶

    When someone links a Telegram account that has no Poracle account, PoracleWeb.NET asks PoracleNG for its registration groups and asks Telegram (getChatMember, with the same bot token as Telegram sign-in) whether that person is in one. Without area security the groups are [telegram] channels. With area security they're each community's telegram.channels, and the account is created in every community whose group holds the person. A member, administrator, creator or restricted user who is still in the chat counts; someone who left or was banned doesn't. The account is then created and filled in exactly as under Always.

    Anyone else keeps waiting and sees the Join the Telegram group button if you set telegram_registration_url. The check fails closed: if the bot isn't in a group, Telegram or PoracleNG can't be reached, or the bot token isn't set, the person waits, and the link itself is never refused for it. The bot has to be a member of each registration group for Telegram to answer about the people in it.

    This checks the person once, at the moment they link. It doesn't do what /poracle also does, which is trigger PoracleNG's immediate membership sync, and PoracleNG's periodic check only looks at accounts that already exist. Someone who joins the group after linking should send /poracle there, as they would without linking. The Discord equivalent (guild roles) isn't built yet.

    Example¶

    Sam signs in with Telegram and has two alarms for Old Town. Sam presses Link Discord, approves the sign-in and returns to the page. With link_register_missing off (the default), the Discord card reads "Waiting to register" until Sam gets the community's role. Once it reads "Receiving alerts", Sam picks Discord under New alarms go to, and the next alarm Sam adds, for Riverside, is delivered on Discord. The two Old Town alarms keep arriving on Telegram until Sam moves them with Deliver to….
