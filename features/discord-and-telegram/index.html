    Discord and Telegram¶

    PoracleNG delivers alerts through two apps, Discord and Telegram, and a server can run either or both. Each app keeps its own accounts. A person who uses both has two PoracleNG accounts, a discord:user and a telegram:user, with their own alarms, areas, location and profiles. PoracleWeb.NET signs people in with either, and linking lets one person treat the two as one.

    This page is for operators deciding what to offer and for members wondering which app to use. The Telegram setup itself is in Telegram.

    What each app does¶

    Discord Telegram
    Sign in to PoracleWeb.NET Discord OAuth2 (see Discord OAuth2) Telegram's login widget, on the one domain set with /setdomain
    Turn sign-in on DISCORD_CLIENT_ID and secret (optional: leave both unset on a Telegram-only site), plus the enable_discord site setting TELEGRAM_ENABLED, TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN, TELEGRAM_BOT_USERNAME, plus the enable_telegram site setting
    PoracleNG account type discord:user telegram:user
    How a person registers A registration channel (!poracle), or a role listed in user_role that grants access without registering Sending /poracle in a registration group the bot is in; optionally /start in a DM with register_on_start
    Bot commands Prefix ! by default (!location, !profile) Prefix / (/location, /profile)
    Direct messages The bot can DM anyone who shares a server with it and allows DMs from server members The bot can DM only someone who has started a chat with it (pressing Start, or allowing messages in the login widget)
    Destinations Webhooks, channels and threads Channels, groups and topics
    Mentions on an alarm A role or a person, on a webhook, channel or thread (the Mention field) Not offered
    Periodic access check [discord] check_role: still in a guild and holding a role [telegram] check_role: still in a registration group
    Area security communities discord = { channels, user_role } telegram = { channels }

    Test notifications, quiet periods, quest summaries, profiles and clean mode work the same on both. The message itself comes from PoracleNG's templates, which can differ per app.

    Destinations are covered in Destinations & delegates. They are never linked: a Telegram group and a Discord channel stay separate destinations, each with its own alarms.

    Registering on each app¶

    PoracleWeb.NET doesn't register anyone by default. A person has to exist in PoracleNG before they can sign in, and signing in with an app they haven't registered on answers "Your account is not registered".

    On Discord, the server's own setup decides. Usually it's a role from the community's Discord server (PoracleNG's user_role, or a community's user_role with area security), or !poracle in a registration channel.

    On Telegram, PoracleNG only registers someone who is a member of a registration group, and only the bot can check that. The usual route is to join the group and send /poracle there. The bot reacts, posts a welcome in the group, and DMs a confirmation, which arrives only once the person has started a chat with the bot. Telegram has every route and what each one says.

    The one exception is Register the other account when linking (link_register_missing). Set to Always, linking creates the missing account on the other app, skipping that app's registration rules. Set to When qualified, it creates a missing Telegram account only for someone who is in a registration group.

    From never used Poracle to both apps¶

    This diagram follows a member who starts with neither account and ends with both linked and receiving alerts. The Telegram branch is the same whether they link first or register first.

    flowchart TD
    A[Never used Poracle] --> B{Which app first?}
    B -->|Discord| C[Get the server's role, or send !poracle in the registration channel]
    B -->|Telegram| D[Join the registration group and send /poracle there]
    D --> D2[Press Start in a DM with the bot]
    C --> E[Sign in to PoracleWeb.NET with that app]
    D2 --> E
    E --> F[Open Linked accounts and link the other app]
    F --> G{Other app registered?}
    G -->|Yes| H[Both sides: Receiving alerts]
    G -->|No, always, or when-qualified and in a registration group| I[Site creates the account, copying areas, location, profiles and places]
    I --> H
    G -->|No, off, or when-qualified and not in a group| J[Other side: Waiting to register]
    J --> K[Register on the other app the usual way]
    K --> H
    H --> L[Choose the default app, then pick the app per alarm]

    What linking ties together¶

    Linking pairs one discord:user with one telegram:user. Nothing is merged inside PoracleNG: there are still two accounts, and PoracleNG still sends each alert from the account that holds the alarm. PoracleWeb.NET does the joining.

    • Alarms are listed together, each marked with its app, and each alarm goes to one app. You pick the app per alarm and can move alarms between apps.
    • Person-level settings (location, saved places, areas, profiles, language, quiet periods, summary schedules, and alerts on or off) are saved to both accounts from the site.
    • Bot commands change one account only. When the two drift apart, PoracleWeb.NET shows the difference and lets the person choose which app's setting to keep.

    The full behaviour is in Linked accounts.

    Choosing what to offer¶

    Running only Discord needs nothing on this page. Adding Telegram means a bot, a registration group and Telegram sign-in on the site; Telegram walks through it. Linking is on by default once both sign-ins work, and the Linked accounts settings cover the choices an operator makes.
